DPMU karyavrit quality register

Every district's monthly DPMU minutes, read by Claude and scored on the tracker's 100-point rubric (Process 20, Agenda 50, Follow-through 30). Add karyavrit links in the Google Sheet; new ones are read once a day.

This site isn't connected to its Google Sheet yet. Add the Apps Script web-app URL to config.js (see README.md).
